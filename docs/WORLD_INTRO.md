# World intro — *Three Body Problem* (fan context)

**This demo is unofficial fan work inspired by Liu Cixin’s *The Three Body Problem*. It is not affiliated with the author, the *Remembrance of Earth's Past* series, or any rights holders.**

## Trisolaris in brief

**Trisolaris** is a world orbited by three suns. Their motion is chaotic: unlike a single star’s steady day and night, three gravities pull one another into patterns that can shift without warning. For life on the surface, the sky is the main enemy.

## Eras that define survival

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Chaotic Era (乱纪元)** | Long, harsh stretches: scorching days, freezing nights, sudden “flying star” heat when one sun dominates the horizon. Civilizations hoard water and seek shelter—or **dehydrate**, folding their bodies into dormant sheets to wait out the worst. |
| **Stable Era (恒纪元)** | Rare, precious calm: milder weather, predictable light, a chance to grow, rebuild, and read what prior cycles left behind. Stable Eras always end; preparing for the return of chaos is part of wisdom. |

## Ideas this demo draws on (not a retelling)

- **Three suns** as an unpredictable clock—not a full physics simulation, but the *feeling* of orbital dread.
- **Cycles of civilization** that rise, fall, and leave ruins and tablets for the next attempt.
- **Hope in cycles**: suffering makes the green, quiet Stable Era feel earned.

## What you do in *Trisolarian Survival*

You are one survivor in the current cycle. Read the sky and forecast, manage heat and water, use the **dehydration pit** when the world burns, and when a **Stable Era** comes, reach the **grove** and the **Final Log**. NPCs and beacons in the demo are **original** characters—homage to the themes of the novels, not copies of their plot or cast.
